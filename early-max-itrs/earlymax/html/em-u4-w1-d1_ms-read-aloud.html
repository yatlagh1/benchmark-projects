Math and Science Read-Aloud
15 MIN

Read-Aloud

Standards

Before Reading (7 MIN.)

Set Purpose

We’re learning about communities. Communities can be different places. Cities, like in Good Morning, City, are a type of community. Let’s learn about cities.

Build Oral Vocabulary

Display the Oral Vocabulary Cards and use the 5-Step Vocabulary Routine on the back of each card to introduce the words: community, crowded, travel.

Build Vocabulary: Refer to the supports on the back of the cards to help students make meaning of new language.

Introduce Good Morning, City

Display Read-Aloud Card 1: Living in a City. Read the title aloud.

Ask: What do you know about cities? Have students Turn and Talk with a partner

Listen in as students discuss. Have 2–3 students share their ideas with the group. If students answer in 2–3 word phrases, remind them to speak in complete sentences. As needed, model student answers as complete sentences and have students repeat.

During Reading (4 MIN.)

Dialogic Read-Aloud

Tell students that you will read four cards about types of communities. Set a purpose for reading the first card. Say: AAs I read, listen to find out about cities

Check for understanding as you interactively read aloud Living in a City.

Paragraph 2: What are buildings in cities like? (tall, close to each other)

Paragraph 3: How do people travel in cities? (train, car, walking).

Paragraph 3: Why do people enjoy living in cities? (they are part of big communities, there is a lot to do)

Observe students as they answer questions. Do students:

  • Speak in complete sentences? (FLD 1.8; ELD 1.2)
  • Answer questions about cities? (FLD 1.7; ELD 1.9)

Use the Observation Record to monitor and assess students’ progress.

After Reading (3 MIN.)

Ask: What are cities like? Have students Turn and Talk with a partner.

Listen in as students discuss. Have 1–2 students share with the group.

Use the scaffolding options below to engage students in multiple-turn conversations.

Extend the Conversation

What are cities like?

Transition (1 MIN.)

Have students transition to Small Group or Centers by stretching as tall as they can like a building in a city.
