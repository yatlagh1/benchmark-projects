Reflection (4 MIN.)

Have students use the Go Around Protocol to tell what they learned about communities. Remind students to use complete sentences. As needed, restate student responses as complete sentences and have students repeat.

Goodbye (1 MIN.)

End the day with a freeze dance. Tell students that when the music is playing, they should dance; when the music stops, they should freeze, or stop dancing.

Instruct students to dance in a way that matches the music. If necessary, provide an example by playing the “classical” Action Clip and model dancing smoothly and slowly.

Observe students as they freeze and dance. Do Students:

  • Move in response to music? (VPA.4.7)
  • Stop movements with control? (PD.1.6)

Use the Observation Record to monitor and assess students’ progress.

Optional Enrichment

Option 1

Throughout the day, direct students’ attention to the size of objects around the classroom and school. Point out large and small objects, then think aloud as you compare them. For example, during mealtime, say: The crackers are small. Look how you can hold them in your hand. The sandwich is big. You need two hands to hold it.

Option 2

Stock the math center with two bins. Use pictures to label one bin for big items, and the other for small items. Instruct students to find objects around the center or classroom and sort them into the correct bin by size. Encourage students to explain their sorting to classmates at the center.
