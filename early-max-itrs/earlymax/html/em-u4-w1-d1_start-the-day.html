Start the Day!

Standards

Morning Message (5 MIN.)

Use the Gathering Protocol and welcome students.

Display and read aloud the Morning Message as you track the print.

Today, we’ll begin learning about communities. People who live in the same place are part of a community.

Say: Let’s watch a video about communities. As you watch, think about what happens in a community.

Play the Unit 4 Introduction Video.

Share Responses

Ask: What did you learn about what happens in a community?

Have students Turn and Talk about what they learned.

Listen in as students discuss. Have 1–2 students share their ideas with the group. As needed, restate students’ answers as complete sentences. Then, have students repeat their answers as complete sentences.

Differentiate Instruction

Support: Replay the Unit 4 Video. Pause when community members are shown. Ask: Who is part of a community? What do they do?

Challenge: Encourage students to make connections to their community. Ask: Who is part of your community? What places do you visit in your community?

Observe students as they Turn and Talk. Do students:

  • Initiate conversations with peers? (FLD 1.8; ELD 1.10)
  • Use complete sentences? (FLD 1.4)

Use the Observation Record to monitor and assess students’ progress.

Daily Schedule (4 MIN.)

Review today’s schedule with students, using the Daily Schedule Routine. Have students:

  • notice any schedule changes
  • use sequence words to tell a partner which centers they plan to visit

Foundational Skills (5 MIN.)

Say: We live in the United States of America. The American flag is a symbol of our country. You may see American flags around our community.

Display “Our Beautiful Flag” on page TK in the Big Book of Songs and Rhymes.

Point to the flag in the photograph. Tell students that this is the flag of the United States of America. Prompt students to tell what they notice about the flag.

Point to the title and read it aloud. Then, read the song aloud while tracking the print.

Say: Some of the words in this song start with /b/. Guide students to identify /b/ as the beginning sound in the word beautiful.

Tell students you will say some words from the song. If the word begins with /b/, they will show a thumbs-up. If the word does not begin with /b/, they will show a thumbs-down.

Say the following words slowly and clearly:

  • bright (thumbs-up)
  • flag (thumbs-down)
  • blue (thumbs-up)
  • stars (thumbs-down)

Have students stand. Use the Echo Song Routine to teach the words and the motions. Play the song. Have students sing the song and do the motions with you.

Transition (1 MIN.)

Have students wave their arms like flags blowing in the breeze as they transition to the Collaborative Lesson.
