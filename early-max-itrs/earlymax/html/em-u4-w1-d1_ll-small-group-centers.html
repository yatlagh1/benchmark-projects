EARLY MAX UNIT 4 WEEK 1 DAY 1 LESSON 4
Language and Literacy Small Group and Centers
4 x
15 MIN

Small Group and Centers

Standards

Work with a small group of students to complete this literacy activity while other students work in Purposeful Play Centers. Rotate through four 15-minute groups in 60 minutes.

Read ABCs in My Community (14 MIN.)

Before

Distribute copies of ABCs in My Community to each student. Have students identify the front cover of the book. Then, have them locate the title on the front cover.

Model pointing to the title and tracking the print as you read it aloud.

Review the alphabet by prompting students to sing the “Alphabet Song" with you.

Tell students that people in communities have different jobs. Explain that each page has a letter of the alphabet and a photograph of someone doing a job.

During

Echo Read: Guide students through a reading of the book. Follow the Echo Read Routine below:

  • Point to the letters uppercase A and lowercase a on page TK. Read the letter names aloud. Say: Uppercase A, lowercase a. Have students point to the letters in their books and repeat the letter names.
  • Say: /a/. Have students repeat the sound.
  • Point to the photograph of the astronaut. Say: astronaut. Have students point to the astronaut and repeat the word.
  • Repeat the Echo Read Routine for the remainder of the book.

After

Connect: What’s the first letter of your name? Find the letter in your book. What do you see on that page?

Differentiate Instruction

Support: Help students identify the first letter of their names by writing their names on the board. Point to the first letter in their name and read it aloud. Ask: What letter? Prompt students to find the letter in their book. Encourage them to use their written name to compare letters. After students find the letter, point to it and Ask: What letter?

Challenge: Have the students extend their thinking by naming another job that begins with the same letter as their first names.

Observe students as look at their book. Do students:

  • Identify the front cover? (FLD 2.7)
  • Identify the first letter of their name? (FLD 2.4)

Transition (1 MIN.)

Have students pretend to be hairdressers, using two fingers like scissors to cut hair as they transition to the next activity.

Resources

Student Text: ABCs in My Community (one per student)

Target Outcomes

Learning Outcomes

Language and Literacy

  • Identify the front cover of a book.
  • Locate the title of a book.
  • Recognize and name uppercase and lowercase letters.
  • Identify words that begin with the same letter as own name.

Social Studies

  • Identify jobs.
